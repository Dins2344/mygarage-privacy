MyGarage Privacy Policy

Effective 6 November 2026

This policy explains how the MyGarage Android app, made by DC Techs, handles your information. MyGarage has no accounts and no server of ours. What you enter stays on your phone, and DC Techs cannot see it.

Who we are

MyGarage is developed by DC Techs. For any privacy question or request, email dinson.cd@gmail.com.

Information stored on your phone

The app stores only what you add to it:

  • Garage details, such as a name, type and address
  • Vehicle details, such as the registration number, make, model, chassis and engine numbers, owner name, financier, purchase details and odometer readings
  • Document details, such as type, number, issuer, dates, amounts and notes
  • Scans and files of your documents, which may show personal details such as your name, address or photo, and vehicle photos
  • Service, fuel and expense records
  • Reminders and their schedules

This information is used only to show your records, suggest document dates, calculate figures such as mileage, and send your reminders. We do not collect it, and we do not sell or share it. The app has no advertising and no analytics of our own.

Permissions

  • Camera: to take vehicle photos and scan documents. Photos stay on your phone unless you share them.
  • Notifications: to show renewal and service reminders. These are scheduled on your phone, not sent from a server.
  • Internet: used by the Google components described below, and to open DigiLocker when you ask. The app does not use it to send your records anywhere.

Photos and files are chosen through Android's system pickers, so the app sees only the items you pick. The app does not use your location, microphone or contacts.

Google services

MyGarage uses Google's ML Kit to read dates from scans, and the document scanner in Google Play services to capture pages. Google states that ML Kit processes images and text on your device and does not send them or the results to its servers.

Google's components do send technical data to Google over an encrypted connection, for diagnostics and usage analytics. This includes device model and Android version, app name and version, identifiers for the app installation or device, performance and error information, image format and size, and which features were used. They may also contact Google to download updates. DC Techs does not receive this data. Google's handling of it is described in the ML Kit data disclosure and the Google Privacy Policy.

Google Play, which installs the app, handles data under its own terms.

Android backup

Your records and scans are excluded from Android's cloud backup. App settings are included, and these can hold the text of scheduled reminders, which may show a document type and registration number. If you have device backup turned on, this text may be saved to your own Google account. You can turn this off in your phone's backup settings.

Because your records and scans are not backed up, they cannot be restored if you lose your phone, change phones or uninstall the app.

When your information leaves the app

This happens only when you choose it. If you tap Share on a document, Android's share sheet sends it to the app you pick, and that app's own privacy terms then apply. Open in DigiLocker opens the DigiLocker website, which is not run by us.

How your information is protected

  • Your records are kept in an encrypted database. Its key is protected by the Android Keystore.
  • Scans and photos are kept in the app's private storage, which Android keeps closed to other apps. They are not separately encrypted.
  • Use a screen lock, because anyone who can unlock your phone can open the app.

Keeping and deleting your information

Your information stays on your phone until you delete it. We hold no copy, so we cannot delete it for you or recover it.

  • When you delete a document, photo or vehicle, its saved scans and photos are deleted from the app's storage straight away.
  • Temporary copies made while scanning or importing can stay in the app's cache until Android clears it or you clear the app's storage.
  • Deleted records are hidden and can stay in the encrypted database until you remove the app's data.
  • To remove everything, uninstall the app or clear its storage in your phone's settings. This cannot be undone.

You can view and correct your information in the app at any time.

Children

MyGarage is meant for adults who own or manage vehicles. It is not directed at anyone under 18.

Not a government app

MyGarage is not affiliated with any government body, DigiLocker or Parivahan.

Changes to this policy

If the way the app handles information changes, we will update this page and its effective date. If we add features such as sign-in or cloud backup, we will update this policy before they are released.
