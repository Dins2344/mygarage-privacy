Delete your MyGarage account

MyGarage, an Android app by DC Techs

You only have a MyGarage account if you chose to sign in with Google to back up and sync your records. If you never signed in, there is nothing on our server to delete. Your records are only on your phone, and uninstalling the app or clearing its storage removes them.

Delete in the app

  1. Open MyGarage and go to Settings.
  2. Tap Account.
  3. Tap "Delete account and cloud data" and confirm.
  4. Choose whether to keep or remove the copy of your data on your phone.

Ask us by email

If you cannot open the app, for example because you lost your phone, email dinson.cd@gmail.com from the Google address you signed in with. Say that you want your MyGarage account deleted. We will delete it within 30 days and reply to confirm.

What is deleted, and when

From the app, these are deleted from our live database and file storage straight away. By email, we do it once we confirm the request is from the account owner. Copies in our providers' backups expire on their schedule (up to 30 days).

  • Your encrypted files (document scans, PDFs and vehicle photos) in cloud storage
  • Your synced records, stored encrypted, including entries for records you had already deleted in the app
  • Your account, including your Google account id and email address, and your encryption key
  • Your sign-in tokens and the list of your cloud files
  • Shared garages your account owns, for everyone in them: their records, files, members and invites. The app warns you first that garages you share are deleted for everyone in them.
  • Your membership in garages other people shared with you. The records you added there stay in those garages.

If a file cannot be deleted, the app shows a message and keeps your account so you can try again.

What is not deleted

  • The copy on your phone, unless you choose to remove it. Shared garages are always removed from the phone. Deleting the account does not delete your Google account.
  • Data you shared out of the app, which is held by whoever you sent it to.
  • Copies in our providers' backups, until they expire.
  • Server logs, which can hold your account id or an IP address. They are kept for up to 30 days.
